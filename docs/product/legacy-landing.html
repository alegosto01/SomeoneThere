What HouseCheck is — and isn't

We want to be precise about the boundaries, so you know exactly what you're paying for.

✓ What we do

  • Check the address exists and the exterior matches the listing
  • Compare listing photos to what's actually there
  • Ask standard landlord/agent authority questions
  • Flag suspicious payment requests and inconsistencies
  • Deliver a private, structured report with a confidence level
  • List what we could not check, clearly

✗ What we don't do

  • Guarantee any rental is safe or the landlord is legitimate
  • Negotiate rent, leases, or deposits
  • Hold or transfer deposits
  • Give legal advice
  • Act as a real estate broker
  • Enter a home without explicit permission
  • Publicly label anyone a scammer

Questions

Does HouseCheck guarantee the rental is safe?
No. HouseCheck collects evidence and risk indicators to help you decide with less uncertainty. It does not guarantee that any rental, landlord, or contract is legitimate or risk-free.
Why not just ask a friend in Madrid to drive past?
You can, and if you trust a friend who is free right now, you should. HouseCheck is for when you want an independent, structured report you can keep — with a clear confidence level and a record of what could and couldn't be checked — rather than a quick favour.
Can you stop me from being scammed?
We can't promise that. What we can do is surface red flags and unresolved concerns early, so you can ask better questions or walk away before paying a deposit. If a report won't be ready before your deadline, we'll say so rather than take your money.
Is this an official inspection or a real estate agency?
No. HouseCheck is an independent evidence service. It is not an official inspection, not a government service, and not a real estate agency. We don't negotiate, handle deposits, or recommend specific properties.
What if my deposit deadline is today?
Be careful — urgency is the most common scam tactic. If we can't deliver even a basic review in time to help, we'd rather you pause and verify independently than pay us under pressure. You can always reach out and we'll be honest about timing.
Important: HouseCheck provides practical verification based on available evidence, risk indicators, and unresolved concerns. A HouseCheck report does not guarantee that a rental listing, landlord, agent, contract, payment request, or rental agreement is legitimate, legally valid, or risk-free. HouseCheck does not provide legal advice. Always use secure payment methods, review contracts carefully, and avoid sending deposits under pressure.
