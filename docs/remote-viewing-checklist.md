# Remote Viewing Visit Checklist

Use this checklist for every MVP pilot visit.

## Before the visit

- Confirm Madrid address, viewing time, and meeting point.
- Confirm the client will join by WhatsApp, Google Meet, or Zoom.
- Save the listing URL and screenshots.
- Note the listing claims: rooms, size, floor, furniture, amenities, price, deposit, bills, and move-in date.
- Confirm who granted access permission.
- Confirm verifier check-in contact and backup admin.
- Charge phone and bring battery pack.
- Test mobile data, camera, microphone, and video-call link.

## Arrival

- Check in with admin.
- Confirm street, building number, and entrance match the claimed address.
- Join the client video call before or at the start of the viewing.
- Explain that the verifier is there only to show the property and observe listing match.

## Permission

- Ask permission before entering.
- Ask permission before filming or showing interior areas on the video call.
- Do not secretly record.
- Do not film people unnecessarily.
- Do not film private documents, IDs, bank details, mail, or personal photos.
- Stop filming if anyone asks.

## Listing match

Compare the live property with the listing:

- Building entrance and common areas
- Bedroom count
- Kitchen
- Bathroom
- Living area
- Windows and natural light
- Furniture and appliances
- General cleanliness and visible condition
- Noise and street context
- Claimed amenities
- Claimed price, deposit, bills, and move-in timing

## Questions to ask

Keep questions factual and neutral:

- What is your role in relation to the flat?
- Who signs the rental contract?
- What is included in the rent?
- What deposit or upfront payment is requested?
- When is the flat available?
- Are there house rules or shared-area rules?
- Are repairs or missing items expected before move-in?

Do not negotiate terms or give legal advice.

## Risk indicators to note

- Refuses live video or interior visibility after access was arranged
- Address or entrance does not match the listing
- Rooms, furniture, or amenities differ materially from the listing
- Pressure to send money immediately
- Person's claimed role is unclear or inconsistent
- Refuses basic factual questions
- Requests unusual payment method
- Says contract or receipt will come only after payment
- Verifier is asked to pay, sign, collect keys, or handle documents

## After the visit

- Leave the area and check in with admin.
- Write the post-visit summary while details are fresh.
- Attach only evidence that was collected with permission.
- Record what could not be checked.
- Record confidence level and unresolved concerns.
- Do not recommend whether the client should rent.
