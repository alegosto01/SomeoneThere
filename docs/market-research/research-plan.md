# Market Research Plan

## Main question

Is a Madrid-focused rental scam verification marketplace commercially viable?

## Research goals

1. Understand whether similar businesses are actively used.
2. Identify which competitors are commercially successful and which are struggling.
3. Discover whether users already pay for remote property visits or rental verification.
4. Estimate whether Madrid has enough demand to support a focused product.
5. Decide whether to build a marketplace, a concierge service, or a hybrid model.

## Research tracks

### 1. Direct competitors

Companies to research:

- Housetective
- Gander Tour
- Viewber
- WeGoLook
- ProxyPics

Questions:

- Are they active today?
- Who pays: tenant, landlord, agency, insurer, platform, or corporate client?
- Are they B2C, B2B, or both?
- What is the average order size?
- How do they recruit and manage local verifiers?
- Is rental verification a core product or only a small use case?

### 2. Task marketplace substitutes

Companies to research:

- Airtasker
- Taskrabbit

Questions:

- Do users actually post rental viewing/property inspection tasks?
- In which cities?
- How much do these tasks cost?
- How often are they completed?
- What trust/safety problems appear in reviews?

### 3. Verified rental marketplaces

Companies to research:

- Spotahome
- HousingAnywhere
- Uniplaces
- Erasmusu
- Flatio
- Wunderflats
- Badi

Questions:

- Do they appear commercially successful?
- What does their verification process include?
- Do they verify external listings? Usually no — this is the HouseCheck gap.
- Are users satisfied with the trust layer?
- What complaints appear repeatedly?

### 4. Madrid/local substitutes

Companies to research:

- Hiliv
- Movely / WeMovely
- Moving2Madrid
- Globexs
- Relomar
- Local relocation consultants
- Facebook group admins
- Real estate agents

Questions:

- Do they help users check properties before arrival?
- Are they expensive full-service alternatives?
- Can HouseCheck be cheaper, faster, and more focused?
