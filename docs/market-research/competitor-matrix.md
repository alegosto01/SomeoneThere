# Competitor Matrix

This file tracks companies and substitutes related to rental verification, remote viewings, property visits, local task marketplaces, and verified rental platforms.

Use this matrix to separate three different types of competitors:

1. **Direct competitors**: services that verify or inspect a rental/property for someone remotely.
2. **Substitutes**: platforms where a user could manually hire someone to inspect a property.
3. **Adjacent platforms**: rental marketplaces that reduce scam risk by verifying their own listings, but do not usually verify external listings from anywhere on the internet.

## Working conclusion

The strongest opportunity for HouseCheck is not to compete directly with full rental marketplaces. The gap is a Madrid-focused, independent, one-off, bring-your-own-listing verification service.

## Metrics to collect for each competitor

For each company, collect:

- Current active/inactive status
- Founded year
- Funding history and investors
- Estimated revenue
- Employee count
- Website traffic
- App downloads, if relevant
- Number of listings, jobs, users, or cities covered
- Public reviews
- Trustpilot, Google Reviews, App Store, Reddit, and forum discussions
- Press coverage
- Signs of growth, decline, pivot, acquisition, or shutdown
- Whether the property verification/viewing use case appears frequent or niche
- Pricing and unit economics
- What HouseCheck can learn from it
