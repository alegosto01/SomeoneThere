# Front-Door Compliance Checklist

> Draft for lawyer review. Covers the public-facing surface (landing page, intake, payment, transactional email) — the highest consumer-protection and misleading-advertising exposure.
> **Blocking**: do not make the payment link public until this checklist is lawyer-approved.

## 1. Landing page / hero

- [ ] No use of "guaranteed," "safe," "certified," "verified-safe," "scam-proof," or "legally verified."
- [ ] The hero states (or links prominently to) the "evidence, not guarantee" framing.
- [ ] "Verification" as a word is used carefully; consider "listing check" / "risk indicators" if the lawyer advises.
- [ ] No urgency cues that mirror scam tactics ("act now," "only today") — see `product-principles.md` fairness principle.
- [ ] Company legal entity, contact email, and registered address are visible (LGDCU/DSA requirement).

## 2. Pricing / checkout

- [ ] Prices clearly state whether they include taxes (IVA/IGIC where applicable).
- [ ] No hidden fees; the price shown is the price paid.
- [ ] The "no refund after delivery" policy is visible **at checkout**, not only in the report disclaimer.
- [ ] Payment timing (charge before work) is disclosed before payment.

## 3. Intake form

- [ ] Link to the privacy policy and data-retention schedule from the intake form.
- [ ] Requester attestation (genuine rental interest) is required, not optional.
- [ ] No collection of sensitive data beyond what the report needs (no ID documents in MVP).

## 4. Transactional emails (confirmation, delivery)

- [ ] Confirmation email restates: this is a verification *report*, not a guarantee.
- [ ] Delivery email includes the evidence-provenance statement.
- [ ] Both emails include a contact and a clear way to request support / deletion.

## 5. Accessibility & vulnerability

- [ ] Page is usable on mobile (target users are on phones).
- [ ] Consider a "free triage" path for clearly hopeless listings (fairness principle, round 2 Risk 10).
- [ ] No dark patterns that push a stressed user to pay.

## Lawyer review checkpoint

- [ ] Lawyer has reviewed the **live** landing page (not just the outline) before the payment link is public.
- [ ] Review logged in `docs/decision-log.md`.
