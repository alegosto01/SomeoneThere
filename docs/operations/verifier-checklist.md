# Verifier Checklist

## Before accepting a job

- Confirm location is reachable.
- Confirm expected duration.
- Confirm whether interior access is required.
- Confirm whether a scheduled viewing is already arranged.
- Confirm payment/payout amount.
- Do not accept if the task feels unsafe.

## Before the visit

- Review listing URL and screenshots.
- Save claimed address.
- Check transport time.
- Charge phone.
- Enable timestamp/location metadata where appropriate.
- Prepare questions for landlord/agent.

## During exterior check

- Confirm street name and building number.
- Take photo/video of street and entrance.
- Check whether building style matches listing.
- Note neighborhood condition and accessibility.
- Do not trespass or enter private areas without permission.

## During interior viewing

- Ask permission before recording.
- Compare listing photos with actual property.
- Check bedroom, kitchen, bathroom, windows, furniture, and common areas.
- Ask about contract, deposit, bills, move-in date, and landlord/agency identity.
- Stay polite and neutral.

## After visit

- Upload evidence.
- Fill report immediately while details are fresh.
- Flag urgent scam indicators.
- Do not give legal guarantees.
