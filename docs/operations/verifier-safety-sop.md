# Verifier Safety SOP Draft

This is an MVP operating draft, not legal or insurance advice. No field visit should be sold until this SOP is reviewed, signed by the verifier, and matched to the required insurance.

## Launch gate

In-person services are blocked until:

- The verifier has accepted this SOP in writing.
- HouseCheck has reviewed field-visit insurance, including civil liability and accident coverage.
- An admin is assigned as the live safety contact for the job.
- The job is daylight-only unless a later lawyer/insurance-reviewed SOP allows otherwise.

## Scope

This SOP applies to exterior/address verification and any viewing attendance. Interior/viewing attendance also requires explicit access permission and a buddy/pair rule.

## Before accepting a job

- Decline any task that feels unsafe or unclear.
- Confirm address, expected duration, daylight timing, and whether interior access is requested.
- Confirm that the verifier can leave at any time and still be paid for a good-faith abort.
- Do not accept tasks involving confrontation, debt collection, deposit exchange, or lease negotiation.

## Check-in cadence

- Text the admin before travel starts.
- Text the admin on arrival near the address.
- Text the admin every 30 minutes during the job.
- Text the admin immediately after leaving the area.
- If a verifier misses a check-in by 10 minutes, the admin calls the verifier.
- If a verifier misses a check-in by 20 minutes, the admin follows the escalation plan.

## Exterior checks

- Daylight-only for MVP.
- Stay in public areas.
- Do not enter gates, stairwells, courtyards, or private areas without explicit permission.
- Do not film people unnecessarily.
- Leave immediately if challenged aggressively or if the situation changes.

## Interior or viewing attendance

- Blocked until safety SOP, insurance, and permission process are signed off.
- Buddy/pair required for interior viewings in MVP.
- Written or message-based permission must exist before entering.
- The verifier never confronts a suspected scammer.
- The verifier does not exchange money, collect keys, sign documents, or negotiate terms.

## Abort rules

The verifier may abort with no penalty when:

- The location, person, or access condition differs from the brief.
- The verifier is pressured to enter without permission.
- The verifier is asked to handle money, keys, contracts, or confrontation.
- The verifier feels unsafe for any reason.

An aborted visit still produces a report describing what was checked, what could not be checked, evidence collected, risk indicators, and unresolved concerns.

## Emergency escalation

- Admin calls the verifier.
- Admin contacts the verifier's emergency contact if no response.
- Admin contacts local emergency services if there is a credible safety concern.
- Admin documents the incident after the verifier is safe.

## Insurance to confirm

Before paid field visits, HouseCheck must confirm:

- Civil liability coverage for field verification activity.
- Accident coverage for verifier travel and site presence.
- Whether verifiers are covered as employees, freelancers, or B2B subcontractors.
- Who pays for each insurance line.
