# Verifier Safety SOP Draft

This is an MVP operating draft, not legal or insurance advice. No Remote Viewing Visit should be sold until this SOP is reviewed, the verifier accepts it in writing, and field-visit insurance is reviewed.

## Scope

This SOP applies to the Remote Viewing Visit package: a trusted local verifier attends a Madrid rental viewing while the client joins by live video call.

The verifier is only the client's eyes and ears. The verifier does not recommend whether the client should rent.

## Launch gate

Remote Viewing Visits are blocked until:

- A backup helper/admin is named with coverage hours.
- The verifier has accepted this SOP in writing.
- SomeoneThere has reviewed field-visit insurance, including civil liability and accident coverage.
- The landlord, agent, or current occupant has given explicit permission for entry.
- The verifier has completed at least one dry run.

## Before accepting a job

- Confirm the address, expected duration, meeting point, and viewing time.
- Confirm the client has a genuine rental interest.
- Confirm the video-call tool and backup phone number.
- Confirm who arranged access and who is expected to be present.
- Decline any task that feels unsafe or unclear.
- Do not accept tasks involving confrontation, deposit exchange, lease negotiation, key collection, document signing, or rent payment.

## Permission rules

- Do not enter private property without explicit permission.
- Ask before filming or recording inside the property.
- Do not secretly record.
- Do not film people unnecessarily.
- Do not film private documents, IDs, bank details, mail, family photos, or other sensitive material.
- Stop filming immediately if asked.

## Check-in cadence

- Text the admin before travel starts.
- Text the admin on arrival near the address.
- Text the admin when entering the property.
- Text the admin immediately after leaving the property.
- Text the admin after leaving the area.
- If a verifier misses a check-in by 10 minutes, the admin calls the verifier.
- If a verifier misses a check-in by 20 minutes, the admin follows the escalation plan.

## During the viewing

- Stay polite, neutral, and non-confrontational.
- Keep the live call focused on showing the flat and comparing it with the listing.
- Let the client ask questions live, but do not negotiate for them.
- Ask only factual, standard questions about the property, access, bills, move-in timing, and the person's claimed role.
- Do not give legal advice.
- Do not say the property is safe, approved, certified, or scam-proof.
- Leave if pressured to pay, sign, collect keys, or confront anyone.

## Verifier must not

- enter without permission
- secretly record
- film people unnecessarily
- film private documents
- negotiate rent
- sign anything
- pay anything
- collect keys
- give legal advice
- guarantee the property is safe
- recommend whether the client should rent
- confront suspected scammers

## Abort rules

The verifier may abort with no penalty when:

- The location, person, access condition, or viewing setup differs from the brief.
- Permission to enter or film is unclear.
- The verifier is pressured to handle money, keys, contracts, or confrontation.
- The verifier feels unsafe for any reason.

An aborted visit can still produce a short summary describing what happened, what could not be checked, evidence collected, risk indicators, and unresolved concerns.

## Emergency escalation

- Admin calls the verifier.
- Admin contacts the verifier's emergency contact if no response.
- Admin contacts local emergency services if there is a credible safety concern.
- Admin documents the incident after the verifier is safe.

## Insurance to confirm

Before paid visits, SomeoneThere must confirm:

- Civil liability coverage for field verification activity.
- Accident coverage for verifier travel and site presence.
- Whether verifiers are covered as employees, freelancers, or B2B subcontractors.
- Who pays for each insurance line.
